HAIR

Hair Fall PRP in Queens, NY

Your own platelet-rich plasma to strengthen thinning hair and reduce shedding.

Growth factors from your own blood are injected into the scalp to support healthier hair growth.

WHO IT’S FOR

Early thinning, a widening parting or excess shedding in men and women.

SESSIONS

4–6 sessions, a month apart, then maintenance.

DOWNTIME

None. Avoid washing hair for 24 hours.

SAFETY NOTE

Blood tests may be advised first to rule out other causes.

What to expect

A small blood draw, plasma prepared in minutes and fine scalp injections. Less shedding within 2–3 months.

Every plan and quote is personalised after your in-person consultation with Dr. Meera Sharma at our Queens clinic.
